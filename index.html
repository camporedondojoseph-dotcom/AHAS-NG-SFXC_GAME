01 • THE GAME

Welcome to the Barangay

Classic Snake gameplay meets Filipino jeepney culture.

🛺

Game Description

Ahas ng SFXC: Jeepney Odyssey Edition is a Filipino-themed arcade game inspired by the classic Snake Game. Players control a jeepney while traveling through the barangay, collecting Filipino food and picking up passengers.

Bring passengers to the Barangay Terminal to earn rewards, build your score, and continue your journey. As the jeepney grows longer, movement becomes more challenging.

Players must avoid bombs, pay attention to traffic conditions, collect special items, and maintain their combo streak while trying to beat the high score.

02 • GAME CONCEPT

Snake Gameplay, Pinoy Style

01

Classic Foundation

The project started from an existing Python Snake Game built using the Turtle module.

02

Filipino Transformation

The original gameplay was enhanced with jeepneys, Filipino food, passengers, barangay terminals, traffic lights, and Filipino-inspired visuals.

03

New Challenge

Bombs, traffic conditions, combo scoring, power-ups, and increasing difficulty make every trip more exciting.

06 • CONTROLS

How to Play

W ↑ Move Up
S ↓ Move Down
A ← Move Left
D → Move Right
P Pause / Resume
SPACE Restart

💡 Driver Tips

  • Collect food to increase your score.
  • Pick up passengers and deliver them to the terminal.
  • Avoid bombs and dangerous obstacles.
  • Watch the traffic system.
  • Use Golden Suman when you need protection.
  • Keep your combo going for better scores.
