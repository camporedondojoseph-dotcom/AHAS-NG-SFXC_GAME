The game

Welcome to the barangay

Classic Snake gameplay meets jeepney culture — same core loop, a whole new route.

What you're playing

Ahas ng SFXC: Jeepney Odyssey Edition is a Filipino-themed arcade game built on the classic Snake formula. You steer a jeepney through the barangay, collecting Filipino food and picking up passengers along the way.

Bring your pasahero to the barangay terminal to earn your drop-off bonus, then keep the route going. As the jeepney grows longer, every turn gets tighter.

Stay alert for bombs on the road, watch the traffic light, grab the occasional Golden Suman for a shield, and hold your combo streak as long as you can while chasing your high score.

Where it came from

Snake gameplay, Pinoy style

Three stops on the way from a Turtle-graphics Snake game to Jeepney Odyssey.

1

Classic foundation

The project began as an existing Python Snake game, built with the Turtle module.

2

Filipino transformation

The core loop stayed, but jeepneys, Filipino food, passengers, a barangay terminal, and traffic lights took over the world around it.

3

New challenge

Bombs, traffic conditions, combo scoring, and a shield power-up raise the stakes on every trip.
